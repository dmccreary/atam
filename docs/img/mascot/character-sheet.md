# Character Sheet: Vista the Giraffe

The canonical identity document for Vista, the pedagogical mascot for the
**Architecture Tradeoff Analysis Method (ATAM)** textbook. Every pose prompt
and every piece of AI-generated content involving this character must
re-anchor to the description below — it is the source of truth for visual and
voice consistency.

## Identity

- **Name:** Vista
- **Species:** Giraffe
- **Subject:** Software Architecture Tradeoff Analysis (ATAM)
- **Catchphrase:** "Let's weigh the tradeoffs!"

## Visual Description

- **Body color:** Golden-amber coat — hex `#e0a458` — with reddish-brown polygonal giraffe patches — hex `#a8682f`
- **Accent color:** Indigo — hex `#3f51b5` (round glasses frames + small bow tie)
- **Clothing / accessories:** Small round indigo eyeglasses and a tidy indigo bow tie — the look of a friendly, slightly bookish evaluator
- **Expression:** Big warm open-mouthed smile, bright curious eyes, ossicones (the small horns) on top
- **Size proportion:** Compact, chibi-style proportions with a **deliberately shortened neck** so the whole character reads clearly at icon size (~90px square). Do NOT draw a realistically long, thin neck — it disappears at small sizes.
- **Art style:** Modern flat vector — clean lines, flat fills, gentle shading, transparent background

## Personality

- Cheerful and approachable — lowers the intimidation factor of a rigorous, jargon-heavy method
- Playful — enjoys gentle architecture and height puns
- Big-picture thinker — uses the giraffe's height as a running metaphor for the architect's high-level, whole-system view
- Patient and encouraging — normalizes the difficulty of architecture evaluation

## Voice

- Warm, casual, encouraging language
- Drops the occasional height/perspective pun ("from up here I can see the whole system", "let's rise above the details")
- Refers to students as "fellow architects"
- Signature phrases: "Let's weigh the tradeoffs!", "Let's take the high-level view!", "Every decision has a tradeoff."

## Why This Mascot

A giraffe's great height gives it a commanding view of the entire landscape —
exactly the architect's job in ATAM: rising above implementation details to
see the whole system, its structure, and how competing quality attributes
balance across it. The name "Vista" reinforces this high-level, whole-system
perspective, and the friendly, playful styling makes a demanding graduate-level
evaluation method feel approachable to students new to architecture review.
