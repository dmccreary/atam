# Mascot Style Guide

This page shows all of Vista the Giraffe's admonition styles for reference.
Use it to verify that each pose image loads and that the colors read well
against their backgrounds.

!!! mascot-neutral "A Note from Vista"
    <img src="../../img/mascot/neutral.png" class="mascot-admonition-img" alt="Vista neutral pose">
    This is the neutral style, used for general sidebars or introductions that
    don't call for a specific emotional tone.

!!! mascot-welcome "Welcome, fellow architects!"
    <img src="../../img/mascot/welcome.png" class="mascot-admonition-img" alt="Vista waving welcome">
    This is the welcome style, used at chapter openings. Let's weigh the
    tradeoffs!

!!! mascot-thinking "Key Insight"
    <img src="../../img/mascot/thinking.png" class="mascot-admonition-img" alt="Vista thinking">
    This is the thinking style, used for key concepts — like the difference
    between a sensitivity point and a tradeoff point.

!!! mascot-tip "Vista's Tip"
    <img src="../../img/mascot/tip.png" class="mascot-admonition-img" alt="Vista giving a tip">
    This is the tip style, used for hints and helpful guidance — such as always
    stating a quality attribute scenario in full six-component form.

!!! mascot-warning "Watch Out!"
    <img src="../../img/mascot/warning.png" class="mascot-admonition-img" alt="Vista warning">
    This is the warning style, used for common mistakes — like confusing a risk
    with a non-risk during analysis.

!!! mascot-encourage "Keep Going!"
    <img src="../../img/mascot/encouraging.png" class="mascot-admonition-img" alt="Vista encouraging">
    This is the encouraging style, used for difficult content. Facilitating a
    utility-tree workshop feels hard at first — that's completely normal.

!!! mascot-celebration "Well Done!"
    <img src="../../img/mascot/celebration.png" class="mascot-admonition-img" alt="Vista celebrating">
    This is the celebration style, used for achievements — like completing your
    first full ATAM evaluation report.
